3. A brief description of the issue, including what you were doing when it happened.
4. Any **error messages** you saw, and **screenshots** if you have them.

## Common questions

**I can't sign in.**  
Confirm you are using the same Towbook credentials you normally use, and that your account is active. If your Towbook account is disabled, access is blocked. If the issue persists, contact us and we'll investigate.

**I'm not receiving job notifications.**  
Check that push notifications are enabled for Lightning Dispatch in your device settings, and that you have an active session (signed in). If you're using the app in a browser, the page must be open to receive alerts.

**I'm not being assigned jobs.**  
Job assignment is location-based. Make sure location/GPS is enabled and that the app is reporting your current location. Also confirm you have selected the services you provide and that you are checked in on Towbook or have an active Lightning Dispatch session.

**A job's arrival time or details look wrong.**  
Estimated arrival times are estimates. If a job's address or details appear incorrect, contact your dispatcher or owner, and let us know so we can investigate.

**I have a payment question.**  
Payment amounts and schedules are set by the business you contract with. Contact them first; if you believe there is a technical error in what the app displays, contact us and we'll verify the records.

**I need to delete my account or my data.**  
See "Deleting your account" below.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your Lightning Dispatch account at any time from within the app: open **Settings → Delete Account** and follow the prompts. Deleting your account removes your Lightning Dispatch profile, uploaded documents, photos, location history, and other data we hold about you.

Please note:
- This does **not** delete your separate **Towbook** account, which is controlled by Towbook and the business you contract with.
- Some records (such as payroll and tax records) are retained only as long as required by law.

If you are unable to sign in and need your account deleted, email us from the address on your account at **lightroad29@gmail.com** with the subject line "Account deletion request," and we will process it.
